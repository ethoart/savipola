# SaviPola - from harvest to local opportunity

**Theme:** Sustainability & Social Impact  
**Tagline:** Sell the harvest. Connect the whole journey.

A farmer in Dambulla has tomatoes ready to sell. A shop buyer in Colombo needs a larger order. A driver has a suitable vehicle, and a local worker can sort and load it. Their shared problem is completing one transaction: volume, price agreement, quality information, transport and labour.

SaviPola connects these steps. A farmer describes a harvest in a familiar language; Gemini on Vertex AI prepares a listing draft for review. A photo review describes visible condition without certifying food safety. Buyers see stock-aware sourcing proposals, transparent costs and an AI explanation of remaining actions. Sellers accept offers before quantities are reserved. Transport and local work bookings support fulfilment; completed jobs build review history.

The distinction is AI linked to a complete local transaction. Deterministic code protects stock and computes costs. Cloud Run hosts the app, Cloud SQL persists transactions and Secret Manager supplies runtime credentials. Deployment source is included; live Google Cloud verification remains required in your project.

Our first pilot would measure coordination time, orders fulfilled, buyer landed cost, farmer proceeds, paid jobs and disputes. Real-world savings and waste reduction have not yet been established.

**Opening:** "Finding a buyer is only the start of selling a harvest. SaviPola connects the rest of the journey: price, stock, quality information, transport and local work. Gemini helps farmers express what they have and buyers understand how to source it."
