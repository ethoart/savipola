SaviPola by Hyper Way connects Sri Lankan farmers, buyers, drivers and local workers in a Sinhala, Tamil and English marketplace. Buyers negotiate harvest orders, farmers reserve stock, and service providers list skills, take bookings and build work histories. The source integrates Firebase Authentication for phone sign-in and Firestore for private saved-harvest shortlists. Cloud Run is the deployment target for the FastAPI app, while Cloud SQL preserves inventory, orders and messages. Gemini through Vertex AI translates P2P messages and drafts replies for user review, keeping originals visible. Structured listing and photo-review APIs assist users without certifying quality. Local tests pass; deployment and live Google integration checks await project and billing setup. Our proposed pilot measures coordination time, fulfilled volume and usability. No measured impact savings are claimed.
